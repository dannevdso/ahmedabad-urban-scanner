METHOD & SOURCES

What this scanner does—and does not—show

Current products

Current study-area products include Google Open Buildings V3 footprints and building-form measures, the full prepared-network centrality layer, and published environmental rasters. Each map legend states its source period and scope.

Bounded and sampled analyses

Some accessibility and tessellation products use bounded areas or sampled origins. They support comparative screening, not claims of complete citywide service, observed use, quality, safety or affordability.

Context layers

GTFS transit and OpenStreetMap public-interest POIs are reference context. Their availability, completeness and dates differ from the main analytical products.

Experimental and modelled products

Building height is modelled, not surveyed. Heat risk is a morphology screening proxy. External shadow, solar and sky-view layers were supplied rather than rerun here, and raw space-syntax fields have not been independently validated. The supplied Development plan is sourced from ARPM Studio, Development Plan for Ahmedabad 2041; it is unverified experimental context, not analytical evidence.

Environmental interpretation

Land-surface temperature is not air or indoor temperature. NDVI and vegetation fraction are not validated tree-canopy or shade measures. The 0–30 m height scale is a display range, not a limit on source values.

Building interpretation

Footprint geometry describes mapped form, not building quality, height, occupancy, ownership or resident characteristics. Click the legend information control for the active product’s specific provenance and caveat.
